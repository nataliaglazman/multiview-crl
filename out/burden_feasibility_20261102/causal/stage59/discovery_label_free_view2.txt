by one Hungarian match on |corr|. 'null' holds that graph fixed and randomises the naming,
so a 'learned' at or below 0 means the recovered structure is not aligned to the truth.
'match |r|' is the range of matched correlations: a low one means the node a factor was
scored at barely tracks it, whatever the F1 says. 'MCC' is their mean, the channel-wise MCC.
With multiple alphas, F1 and SHD use each row's best-F1 alpha, selected against the truth (optimistic).
The JSON alpha_sweep retains every alpha for comparisons at a prespecified threshold.
'mix' reruns the panel on random mixings of the row's channels (mean±sd over draws): what the
same block gives with no rule for choosing its channels, with the same best-alpha selection.
Only the recorded columns are mixed; the other channels stay fixed. Read F1 and MCC against it.
